Galaxies & Human Discovery

Observable Universe Estimate
A few hundred billion to ~2 trillion

Spans roughly 93 billion light-years across. Newer cosmic optical light measurements favor the lower-to-mid hundreds of billions, while deep-field models reach up to ~2 trillion.

How Many Humans Have Discovered
Tens of millions catalogued (1.5B+ in sight)

Major sky surveys catalogue tens of millions—ESA’s Euclid logged ~26M galaxies in its first release and plans to image 1.5+ billion.

What We Know About Them
  • Milky Way: Barred spiral with 100–400B stars, ~100,000 ly wide, and 50+ dwarf companions.
  • Andromeda (M31): Spiral ~2.5M ly away with ~1T stars, gradually approaching a merger.
  • Clusters & Web: Grouped in the Local Group, Virgo Cluster, and vast superclusters along the cosmic web.
